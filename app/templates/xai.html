1. Multi-Head Attention & Saliency

Computes dot-product attention across multiple attention heads:
Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QKᵀ / √d_k) V

Token saliency is derived via the gradient of the deceptive logit with respect to token embeddings:
Saliency(w_i) = w_i · ∇_{w_i} L(fake)

Identifies promotional keywords, hyperbolic triggers, and unnatural repetitions.

2. Stylometric Fingerprinting

Empirically grounded in deceptive opinion spam research (Ott et al. 2011). Extracts 20 quantitative linguistic dimensions:

  • Exclamation Density: Inflation of artificial hype.
  • All-Caps Word Ratio: Urgency engineering.
  • First-Person Pronouns: Feigned personal presence.
  • Type-Token Ratio (TTR): Lexical repetitiveness.
  • Flesch Reading Ease: Syntactic formulaicity.

3. Sentiment-Rating Dissonance

Evaluates structural coherence between stated customer star rating and lexical sentiment valence:
Dissonance = |Polarity - (Rating - 3.0)/2.0|

Immediately flags click-farm bot inversion bugs, review bombing campaigns, and sarcastic counterfeit reviews.
